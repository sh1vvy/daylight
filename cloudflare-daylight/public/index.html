BEFORE YOU PRESS PLAY

A few details.

Installation, accounts and audio sources.

How do I install Daylight?+

Download the APK and open it on your Android phone. Allow your browser to install apps if Android asks, then follow the installer. Install updates over the matching Stable or Dev app to keep its data.

Do I need to sign in?+

You can browse and play music without signing in. Connect Google / YouTube Music for your account library, better recommendations, Play my mix and shared listening. Each Jam participant needs to sign in.

Where does the audio come from?+

Standard streaming uses YouTube Music. Optional Lossless and Hi-Res Lossless in Dev use matching FLAC recordings from a separate community source through a Monochrome-compatible integration. They are not converted YouTube streams. Availability varies, and the player labels the quality actually playing.

Does Spotify provide the playback?+

Spotify integration brings in playlists. Daylight matches their tracks to YouTube Music for playback. Played Spotify playlists can appear in Library and Listen again.

Stable or Dev?+

Stable 0.2.1 is the main download. Dev 0.2.2-dev.8 includes the newest features shown here, with beta features available to test. Keep updates within your chosen channel.

Can the app update itself?+

Daylight can offer a newer version in the app. Download it and confirm the Android installer. All builds and release notes are also available on GitHub.
